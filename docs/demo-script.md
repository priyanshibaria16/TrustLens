# TrustLens Professor Demonstration Script

## 1. Introduction
*“Welcome to TrustLens, an Explainable NLP framework designed to assess the textual trustworthiness of online reviews. Unlike standard ML classification that simply outputs ‘True’ or ‘False’, TrustLens analyzes linguistic signals and explains exactly why a score was given.”*

## 2. High-Signal Review Demo
1. Open TrustLens Home Page (Analyzer).
2. Click the **"High Signal"** example button.
   *Text: "I stayed at this hotel for four nights with my family. The room was clean and the staff responded quickly when the air conditioner stopped working on the second evening. Breakfast had reasonable options, although the coffee was disappointing. The hotel is about ten minutes from the main market."*
3. Click **Analyze Review**.
4. **Point out on the Results Page:**
   - **Trust Score:** (Typically High, ~85+)
   - **Feature Breakdown:** High Specificity & Concrete Details, High Personal Experience, Moderate Evidence.
   - **Contributors:** Point out the positive signals found (e.g., specific details, first-person experience).
   - **Explanation:** Read the dynamic explanation generated by the framework.

## 3. Low-Signal / Promotional Review Demo
1. Click **Back to Analyzer**.
2. Click the **"Low Signal"** example button.
   *Text: "AMAZING!!! BEST HOTEL EVER!!! 100% PERFECT!!! EVERYONE MUST STAY HERE!!! THE BEST HOTEL IN THE WORLD!!! ABSOLUTELY PERFECT!!!"*
3. Click **Analyze Review**.
4. **Point out on the Results Page:**
   - **Trust Score:** (Typically Low, ~20-30)
   - **Feature Breakdown:** High Promotional Language, High Exaggeration.
   - **Contributors:** Point out the negative signals (excessive superlatives, repeated punctuation).
   - **Explanation:** Note how the explanation identifies the review as having suspicious stylistic patterns lacking concrete details.

## 4. Addressing Limitations
*“Notice the warning banner at the bottom. The system explicitly states that it is estimating textual trustworthiness, not factual truth. It’s analyzing writing styles associated with the Ott dataset’s deceptive labels.”*

1. Navigate to the **Analytics** page.
2. Show the overall distribution of Trust Levels across all reviews analyzed so far in the session.
3. Conclude by reiterating the project's goal of merging Explainable AI with NLP for deceptive text assessment.
